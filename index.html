About

Chief Macro Strategist and Portfolio Manager at Ascend Investment Management LLC, a $1.2B AUM hedge fund focused on global macro and special situations investing.

With 11+ years in investment management, Emma brings deep expertise across global macro, special situations, private equity/venture capital consulting, and equity research. She is a regular contributor to Fox Business's "Making Money with Charles Payne" and is an active participant in the investment community.

Emma holds an MS in Data Science from NYU (specializing in Computer Science and Mathematical Optimization, GPA 3.8) and an MS in Accounting & Finance plus Master of Taxation from Notre Dame's Mendoza College of Business. She is a CFA charterholder, CPA, Certificate in Quantitative Finance (CQF) holder, and Certified Insolvency & Restructuring Advisor (CIRA).

A published author with 17,000+ LinkedIn followers, Emma's research and commentary focus on macro drivers, market technicals, and complex financial structures in global markets.
